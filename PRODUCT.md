# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML, CSS and JavaScript with no build step, in English and Greek (EN/EL switch). The presenter speaks in both languages too: an English video and a Greek video, each with captions in its own language.

## Users

Business owners who need a website and are deciding who should design it. They are in Cyprus or anywhere else, and most arrive on a phone, often from a social link or a shared message.

## Product Purpose

webbiespirit is a web design studio. This repository holds its sponsor page: a short welcome from an on-camera presenter that introduces the studio and asks the visitor to get in touch about a site of their own. Success means a visitor understands what webbiespirit does and starts a conversation by email.

## Positioning

webbiespirit designs a free concept preview of a business's new website first, so the owner sees their own site before deciding anything.

## Operating Context

The studio is based in Cyprus and also works remotely with businesses anywhere. Contact happens by email at webbiespirit@gmail.com. The user approved showing this address publicly. The studio's main website is live at webbiespirit.com, and its Instagram account is @webbiespirit (https://www.instagram.com/webbiespirit/). The main site is a separate site. This page is a campaign page beside it and never replaces it.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Confirmed offer: a free concept preview of the new website; based in Cyprus; works worldwide and remotely.
- Not confirmed, so never claim it: "custom" sites, a local-businesses-only focus, prices, turnaround times, guarantees, client names, testimonials, ratings, or past results.
- The presenter is a fictional person made for this page. By the owner's decision (2026-10-12), no surface labels how she was made. She presents the studio. She never poses as a client, so the page shows no reviews or endorsements in her name.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: webbiespirit, set in lowercase.
- The presenter wears cobalt blue, and the brand world is built from that cobalt.
- No logo, palette or typeface was supplied at the start. On 2026-10-19 the owner asked for a new logo for Instagram: gold and white only, built on a W signature, and unlike anything seen before. It is the W signature in a spun-gold sun, in `social/logo/` (see `social/logo.md`). The campaign page still carries its small sun-disc mark.

## Evidence on Hand

- Presenter photograph and talking videos, made for this page.
- No portfolio, testimonials, client logos or pricing are available. Do not fabricate them.

## Product Principles

1. Show first, then ask: the free preview is the offer, and the page should feel like one.
2. Every claim is one the studio confirmed.
3. Phone first: the page is judged on a phone, with one thumb, in daylight.
4. Honest presenter: warm and welcoming, and never posing as a client or a reviewer.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Captions on all spoken video in English and Greek; video never autoplays with sound; reduced-motion respected; WCAG 2.2 AA contrast.
